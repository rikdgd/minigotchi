# How to play

> This document describes the game mechanics of the Minigotchi game and does NOT explain how the game
> can be built/run. To read about that see ["README"](README.md).

The Minigotchi game is a game in which you take care of a virtual pet by feeding it and letting it play.
However, you will also have to give your pet enough sleep and take care of it when it gets sick. Each creature
has a different *random* personality, meaning they are all different.

The game has a couple of core mechanics which will all be explained here, these are the following:
- Interactions
    - Feeding
    - Playing
    - Sleeping
    - Healing & sickness
- Death
- Personality
- Coins
- Purchasable items


## Interactions

In order to take care of your pet you will need to interact with it. There are a total of
4 different interactions: **feeding, playing, sleeping** and **healing**. Each has its own purpose
and is equally important.

### Feeding
Your pet needs to be fed to prevent it from starving. There are 3 different kinds of food you can give
your pet: *soup, cookie* and *burger*. Soup grants the least amount of *food* points, but also has a low
chance of making your pet sick. A burger however grant the most food points, but has a high chance of making
your pet unwell.

Feeding your pet is not entirely without risk, every now again your pet might get sick! More details
about sickness will be explained later on.

### Playing
Your pet will literally die of boredom when you don't play with it, so it's best that you do. However, playing
costs energy, which you will have to recover by letting your pet sleep.

Just like with feeding, each playing interaction grants a different amount of *Joy* points. 

### Sleeping
In order to restore energy your pet needs to sleep. By pressing the *"Sleep"* button, you can let your pet sleep
or wake it up. Restoring energy takes quite some time, so make sure you let your pet sleep enough.

### Healing & sickness
When feeding your pet there is a possibility that it gets sick. If sickness is left untreated your pet will
die within a couple of minutes or even seconds. You will know that your creature is sick when it stays centered
in the screen and starts vibrating. 

In order to heal your pet you simply have to press the *"Heal"* button. This will cure the sickness,
but **does not** restore your pet's health, this will restore slowly over time.


## Death
Of course taking care of a pet comes with the risk of it dying. If you do not take care if your pet it will
eventually die. There are three ways on which a pet can die, so try to prevent them.

1. **Stat reaches 0** - Once either the *Food*, *Joy* or *Health* value reaches 0.
2. **To low total** - If the combined value of the *Food* and *Joy* values gets to low.
3. **Sickness** - Sickness quickly drains your pet's *Health* value, killing it when it reaches 0.


## Personality

As mentioned in the **interactions** explanation each pet has a different personality. This personality is
completely random and has nothing to do with the kind of pet the user has picked when creating a new pet.
A pet's personality decides what **feeding** and **playing** interaction it does and doesn't like.

It is a good idea to remember what your pet likes and dislikes, since it has a big influence on the friendship
between you and your pet. A creature's personality is static and will not change, meaning that what each pet
likes/dislikes also does not change.


## Coins

The Minigotchi game has in-game currency simply called coins. These coins can be used to buy two different types of
items: **creature colors** and **backgrounds**, which will be discussed in more detail later on. 

Most importantly there are two ways in which you can receive coins:
1. **Keeping your pet alive** - For each day your pet survives, you gain 1 coin.
2. **Picking up dropped coins** - When the pet has developed enough *love* for the player, coins will start to appear
  on the screen. Your pet will not collect these coins on its own, you will need to guide your pet towards it by
  using your mouse cursor.
  
  
## Purchasable items

Once you have collected some coins they can be spent in the shop. Here you can buy two different types of
items:
- **Creature colors** - These can be bought to change the color of your pet.
- **Backgrounds** - Backgrounds can be bought to draw a background in the pet's playing area.

After an item has been purchased it can be equipped in the shop page, this is done by simply clicking on the item.

> It is always possible to switch back to the default background and color, since these are added to the inventory
> as items from the beginning of the game. It is not possible to refund items however, so think before you buy.
